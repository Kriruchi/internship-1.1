## 📌 Project Overview
This dashboard analyzes Online Retail sales data to track quantity sold and revenue performance. It provides insights into daily sales trends and product-wise performance to help business owners make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Objective
To build an interactive dashboard that visualizes:
- Quantity and revenue by Day
- Quantity and revenue by Product
- Total quantity sold by Product

## 📈 Visuals Used

### 1. Quantity and Revenue by Day (Combo Chart - Left)
- X-Axis: Day of Month (1-31)
- Y-Axis: Quantity (Bar) and Revenue (Line)
- Insight: Sales peaked on Day 22 (12 units) and Revenue peaked on Day 25 (₹6,16,498). Shows daily business trend.

### 2. Quantity and Revenue by Product (Area Chart - Top Right)
- Products: Smartphone, Office Chair, Laptop, Headphones, etc.
- Insight: Smartphone generated highest quantity (80+) and Office Chair generated good revenue (₹1,60,359). Laptop shows a revenue spike (₹2,15,656) despite low quantity - indicates high-value product.

### 3. Quantity Sold by Product (Bar Chart - Bottom Right)
- Top Seller: Smartphone - 82 units
- 2nd: Office Chair - 70 units
- Least: Keyboard & Backpack - 26 units each
- Clearly shows which products are in demand.

## 🔑 Key Insights
1.  Top Performing Product: Smartphone is the best-seller (82 units sold).
2.  High-Value Product: Laptop generates highest revenue per unit.
3.  Sales Trend: Mid-month (Day 15-25) shows highest sales volume
